Resources used

Items

Cost = what one unit costs you to acquire/make (Groschen). Price = what you sell it for. Profit = price − cost · Margin = profit ÷ price · ROI = profit ÷ cost. Break-even = units needed to cover the fixed costs using that item alone. "Sell 1" lowers stock, raises units sold. Everything saves automatically in this browser.

Fixed costs

Rent, stall fees, taxes, wages, guard protection… anything you pay regardless of sales. The "Suggested price" column in the items table uses your target margin: cost ÷ (1 − margin).
